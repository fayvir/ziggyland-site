Ziggyland support

Something not working, or an idea? Write to info.ziggyland@gmail.com and say which device you use.

Common questions

How do I get to Settings?

Tap the gear at the top right of the screen, then answer the grown-ups' question. Settings is where you add players, set levels, turn lands on or off and change sound.

I bought the unlock but the lands are still locked

In Settings, tap Restore purchase at the top of the Players tab. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple ID that bought it and is online.

Can I share the unlock with my family?

The unlock covers every child on a device. On other devices signed in to the same Apple ID, tap Restore purchase.

How do I delete my child's data?

Everything stays on the device. Remove a player in Settings > Players, or delete the app. See the privacy policy.

Does it need the internet?

No. The games play offline. Buying or restoring the unlock needs a connection.
