Ziggyland privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Ziggyland is a learning game for children aged 3 to 9, for iPhone and iPad. In short: the app collects nothing. Nothing about your child or you is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

When you add a player, the app keeps what you enter (a first name or nickname, a birth date, an animal character and, if you choose, girl or boy for which prizes to offer) and the child's progress in the games. All of it is stored only on the device, inside the app. It is not uploaded, backed up to our servers or shared. We have no servers.

To delete it, remove a player in Settings > Players, or delete the app, which removes everything it stored.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No links out of the app, chat or social features for children.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through the Apple App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we receive no payment details and do not learn who bought it. The app only asks the App Store whether the unlock is owned on this Apple ID. Purchases are behind a grown-ups' check.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children. This is meant to meet the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and Apple's Kids Category rules.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app changes, and the date above will change with it.
